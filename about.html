About Us

BloomIntel is a leading talent acquisition and workforce solutions company dedicated to connecting exceptional talent with outstanding career opportunities across multiple industries.

BloomIntel is a leading talent acquisition and workforce solutions company dedicated to connecting exceptional talent with outstanding career opportunities across IT, Non-IT, Engineering, Healthcare, Finance, BPO, Retail, Manufacturing, and emerging industries.

With our headquarters in the United Kingdom and a strong operational presence in Bengaluru, India, we combine global recruitment expertise with deep local market knowledge to provide intelligent hiring solutions for businesses of all sizes.

At BloomIntel, we believe that the right people are the foundation of every successful organization. Our experienced recruitment specialists leverage industry insights, advanced sourcing strategies, and a personalized approach to identify professionals who not only possess the required skills but also align with an organization's culture and long-term vision.

Whether you're a company searching for exceptional talent or a professional looking for your next career opportunity, BloomIntel is committed to making every connection meaningful, efficient, and successful.
